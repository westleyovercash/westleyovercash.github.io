I stayed up past my bedtime. Before rolling over on my right side, I got up to check my phone. 

Maybe it won't hurt to open Instagram, I thought. She appeared first: big blonde hair and a smile that could cure anything. The years under her photo tried to bind together an end and a beginning.  

I searched for "Coat of Many Colors" and hit share. A sniffle stretched into a snort.
