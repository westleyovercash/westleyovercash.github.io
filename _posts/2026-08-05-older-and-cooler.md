They turned to me, eyes peering out from under their mops. The one on the right stood only as tall as my shoulder. 

The shorter one did all the talking. "We'll pay you," he pleaded, his voice cracking through a thick Ukrainian accent. They wanted the cider as a gift for a friend. I laughed and told them no. 

At the end of the aisle I stopped and looked at my favorite bottle of Primitivo.
