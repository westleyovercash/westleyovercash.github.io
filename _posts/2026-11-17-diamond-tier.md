I unbuttoned my coat and sat down on the couch in the lobby of the Fairmont Golden. A "reserved" placard on the table told me to move.

I settled next to the see-through fireplace. To my left sat three men in ill-fitting suits exchanging *ty vole*s while discussing the route to the airport. One of them picked up his cup of coffee and keys from the table and walked through the line of fans outside onto his bus.

I peered through the fireplace to the other side of the lobby. The flames revealed tan, dark-haired men in burgundy Adidas tracksuits, all with biceps that bulged over the arms of their chairs. Absorbed in the screens in their laps, they pretended to chat with each other. A man in a matching burgundy tracksuit with an earpiece scanned the lobby and occasionally spoke into his hand.

A waiter who looked like he belonged in high school appeared at my side and asked if I wanted a coffee. I wondered if it was free and sharply said "no."

Then they emerged from the corridor of elevators. "Westley," one of them called out.
