The wheels of her shopping caddy clacked against the cobblestone. A marigold was pinned on the side. 

I leaned in from a distance. "Do you need help with that?" I asked, nodding toward the sets of stairs leading into the vestibule. I didn't wait for a response.

"My knees hurt so bad," she said, gripping the handrail. I positioned myself in front of her just in case.
