The officer examined my passport, then glared at me. He swiped my documents and nodded me along. 

I ran through Schiphol, exchanging words with a man who blocked the escalator. A high pulse warning beeped again on my watch. Gate B15 stood abandoned except for the two in Dutch blue. A dot matrix printer hurried to finish the manifest. 

KLM's first A220 huddled in front of the window. "You must go to Transfer Desk T2," they told me as they packed up their things.
