I rode the short escalator up into the reconstructed square at Jiřího z Poděbrad. I imagined even more green than when I lived beside it.

Searing sun rays pelted the concrete, spreading its brutal whiteness in every direction. Benches languished unoccupied as people floated through it. Tiny trees offered nothing but their tensed up branches.   

I found my sunglasses atop my head and shielded my eyes.
