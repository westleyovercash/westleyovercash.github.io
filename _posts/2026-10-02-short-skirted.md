I counted three of them, all in pressed white dress shirts with black name tags. They drifted in figure eights on the platform at Můstek. Most people rushed away from them as if on fast forward. Some shook their heads. Those who didn't understand waved them off. 

A teenage girl with dyed blond hair, a short skirt, and high boots leaned against a polished marble column. All three boys looked at each other. The shortest of them strode toward the girl and extended his hand. She blushed and told him she didn't speak English.    

The next train came. The boys got on through different doors and started canvassing like ticket inspectors.
