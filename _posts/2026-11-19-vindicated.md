We used to see each other often at the gym. She taught me the difference between a decent and a lousy squat, that my hamstrings are way too tight, and that I probably don't really know what I'm doing at the gym anyway.

On a Friday at the end of last summer, Tereza disappeared. Her anger with owner Ivan had spread from a pimple into full-blown acne. She left with her gear and her resentment, but not her kilo-size packets of tuna. Occasionally I'd hear some of the regulars whisper reasons.

We ran into each other on Wednesday at Národní. The light reflected off her ultra-shiny forehead and cheeks. "I just came from a facial," she said.
