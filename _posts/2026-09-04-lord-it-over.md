"I hate duck," I said. "But you'll eat this. Sapo has the best!" Honza said, twizzling his moustache.  

Five of us from the office crammed ourselves into Honza's decades-old Volkswagen. I got the hump seat in the back, with a sometimes view of Štěpán's azure eyes. Our small talk intertwined over the music and rushing air. 

Near Sapo, all the cars stopped on the expressway. Honza looked at me in the rearview mirror from under his Supreme hat as he stole the emergency lane. I grabbed onto both headrests.
