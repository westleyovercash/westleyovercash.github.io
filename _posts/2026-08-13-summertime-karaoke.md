title: Summertime karaoke
date: 2026-08-13
---
Sleep came deep. Back-to-back days of roasting temperatures made me wonder how many survivable summers remain.

At 00.53 I awoke to a slurred "I will always love you." Drunken girls swayed in the window of the nearby Airbnb. They sang louder to make it sound more believable. A couple passing by joined in. 

I looked up at the street light and closed my window.
