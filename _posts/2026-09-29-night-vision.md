Serve her granules in the morning, and wet food in the evening. Tomáš's brownish-green eyes locked onto mine to double-check I understood. 

Maja introduced herself with a hiss. 

At mealtime, she brushed back and forth against my leg. She spent the rest of the day huddled under the bed, staring at me. During the night, she nestled in the sock drawer. 

She pounced at something and landed with a thud. I yelled at her to stop.
