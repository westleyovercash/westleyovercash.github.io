The coffee almost burned my tongue. To distract it, I gobbled down a piece of homemade plum cake.

We gifted each other with a cordial "ahoj." He sipped on his coffee too. He stretched for a bit, then continued on with his trainer and his routine. 

Our eyes met again, but we both looked away. His hair gleamed greyer and the lenses in his glasses glowed redder than I remembered.
