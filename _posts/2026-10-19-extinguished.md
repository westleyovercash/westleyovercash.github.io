The weather app mistakenly imagined sun for them. But in Karlovy Vary in mid-October even sunny days carry an impending darkness. They gathered half-willingly at the cemetery. They all dressed in black, except for Irena. She wore unapologetic bright red. She had yielded enough to František, including giving him the three children he had stopped taking care of at some point.

For František the location atop the hill—with a view of the Colonnade—would be an upgrade. He'd get an exclusive address alongside some of the most famous barons and magnates from across Central Europe. David lowered the urn into the ground. Irena  nodded to the children from behind her dark sunglasses as she exhaled a plume of smoke. She tossed her spent cigarette and crushed it into the ground with the toe of her moccasin. 

"Hasta la vista," she said.
