The conveyor belt presented sushi and onion rings. A suspected testicle or eyeball was actually lychee.

A customer came through the front door dressed in an olive-green field jacket. He coaxed his remaining strands of hair across his forehead and walked behind me to a free table. 

A YouTube ad interrupted Přemek's news about finding a bottle of wine in his son's room. I turned around to settle my internal bet that it was the man seated behind me, then grabbed a Philadelphia roll. In midbite, the sound of a woman climaxing belted out. Přemek and I looked at each other and froze. 

A middle-aged woman with a wrinkly, exhausted face opened the door. Standing halfway inside, she scanned the room. Her eyes landed on the man behind me.
