Mom's face escaped the frame, so I asked her to slide the iPad over. With her and Dad centered, we began talking about the book. Dad said he only had a few pages left to read. Mom had finished it over the weekend.

I wanted them to love *Help, Thanks, Wow* as much as I did. They just needed to get to the page where Anne writes about devout Christians mistakenly calling God by the name of "Howard," as in "Our Father, who art in heaven, Howard be thy name." They'd laugh because that was my grandpa's name. And maybe they'd stick with it.

"Did you have any favorite parts?" I asked.

"I had to look up one word," Dad said as he broke away from the screen to get the lined legal pad. He began spelling out loud: "v-i-c-h-y."

"Vichyssoise," I said, then raised my fists and exhaled a winning "Yaaaaas! Only Anne could describe fog that way."
