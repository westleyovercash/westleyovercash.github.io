Hilti kicked the can of Lion Lager to keep it inside my new circle of buddies. They called the game Alti-Can.

Jumpers from the South African National Skydiving Team played late into the night there on Maiden's Cove beach. After a final round of Alti-Can, they tugged in the lines of fresh fish, butterflied them with a camping knife, and slapped them onto hot stone grills. A medley of new stars offered enough light to test for doneness.

"You'll be going up at the weekend... but tandem," Hilti said. Then he winked and smacked me on the shoulder.
