He parked his mobility scooter in front of the tabák. He leaned up towards the window and extended his arm. His left hand clung to a wad of money, which he exchanged for a small stack of green cards.

With his right hand he found his reading glasses atop his flat cap and adjusted them on his nose. He held the cards close to his chest for a moment, closed his eyes, and exhaled. With the focus of an archaeologist, he scratched the top card with a 20 Kč coin.

People on the sidewalk took turns squeezing around his perch.  He blew off the lottery dust, then pulled out the next card.
