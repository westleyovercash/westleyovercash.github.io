The light moved up and down, capturing every detail of my face. The machine returned my passport and opened the gate. 

At the security checkpoint, they commanded me to place each item separately. On the other side of the metal detector a bald man with an earring inquired about my toiletry bag. As I struggled to find the loops for my belt, his younger, bearded colleague smiled. He stepped forward and asked if he could open it. 

After examining the contents, he looked up at me and batted his turquoise eyes. "What caused it?" I asked.
