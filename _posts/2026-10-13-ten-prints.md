The police arrived, along with a forensic detective. In the space next to the Ping-Pong table, I pointed to all the desks with dangling cables.

We walked to my office and opened the door to the adjacent supply closet. Shards of glass littered the floor under the small ventilation window. The forensic detective joked about the world's smallest person as he knelt and began unpacking his kit. The other officers whispered to each other in a huddle.

Then the one in charge approached me and said, "You must come with us." I turned my head and mouthed to my wide-eyed colleagues, "it's okay."
