The giant gray dome invaded the middle of the school library. It came once a year. 

Over the sound of a fan whirring, Mrs. Newton warned us to take off our shoes. Otherwise, we might tear it. Inside, a breeze floated the smell of popcorn feet. Stars splashed above in the darkness. We sat Indian style and traveled with her into the Milky Way.  

Another class waited its turn. I crawled out last.
