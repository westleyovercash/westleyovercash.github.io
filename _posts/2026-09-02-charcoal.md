I never spent time alone with Grandma until that day. In her rust-colored kitchen, she sat me down with a piece of paper and crayons. 

Grandma dolloped biscuit dough onto the baking sheet and deposited it in the oven. We talked about Papa and the airplanes he built while I sketched and she sipped apple juice from a fat, round glass. 

Black smoke zigzagged its way out of the oven. Grandma jumped up, grabbed the hot mitts, and opened the oven. She laughed so hard she fell back into the chair.
