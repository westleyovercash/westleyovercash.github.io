The bowl held a few uneaten bits of chicken and tofu soup. On the adjacent plate, only the hand-painted flowers remained.

The hosts offered me more red-braised beef and stir-fried egg. My hands waved no, then pointed to my belly. Was it rude? Peter asked to clear the table. One by one, he placed the used dishes in the sink.

Peter ran a trickle of water and turned it off. Holding the sponge against the first bowl, he guided his arm in a clockwise stroke. Strands of thinning gray hair fell over his forehead as he leaned into the silent chant. Once the bowl was clean, he set it in the drying rack and reached for the next.
