"I think she's drunk," I told my number two. "That's impossible—she just earned her wings yesterday," he said.

She began prepping the galley. Ice spilled out of the bucket, and a faint smell of vodka lingered on top of exhaust and jet fuel. I asked her to check the lavatories at the rear of the 717. Her hips ricocheted against the armrests as she walked down the aisle. She grabbed a seatback.

I leaned in through the cockpit door. The captain and first officer turned from their paperwork and trained their eyes on me. I explained to them what I thought I couldn't have seen.

"It's your call," the captain replied.
