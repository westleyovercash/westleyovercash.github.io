title: Folded 
date: 2026-10-05
---
My friend Flora mentioned that she often notices the woman after closing up shop. She'll pause to chat and sometimes give her small coins. 

The woman wore baggy black house pants with faded anchors, untied pink shoes, and a blue stocking cap. An oversized winter coat half-hid her Z-shaped body. She tried but couldn't lift her chin off her chest. Her arms flailed at a group of office workers on their lunch break. They swerved like a school of fish to the other side of the street, their arms folded and lips curled. 

A young couple stopped and leaned in closer to the woman. I heard them reply: "When you see the next curb, take a right."
