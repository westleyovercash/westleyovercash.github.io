title: Raggedy Ann
date: 2026-11-03
---
The last time we met, her hand-sized radio pumped out dance music. Her hips twirled, eyes rolled back in her head. When she exited the tram, she left a skid mark on the seat.

I met her again as I turned past the pizzeria on my way to the tram stop. She rocked back and forth, like a shy schoolgirl. She tipped the plastic Coke bottle back, struggling to align it with her lips. A single dreadlock fell from its tied-up crown and hit the bright red circle of lipstick drawn on her left cheek. Her stubble showed more than usual.

I gawked. Her pin-sized pupils saw it. She tucked back the stray dreadlock and turned away.
