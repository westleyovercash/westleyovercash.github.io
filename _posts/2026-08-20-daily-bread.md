I tossed my receipt in the bin and headed toward the exit. A handful of people waited in the first checkout line. The blonde-headed man with a vacation-looking tan scooched past them. 

His sinking Adidas track pants revealed tufts of pubic hair, while a bottle of Tuzemák poked out from the front pocket of his hoodie. An employee with a headset dashed to the door and gestured him to stop. He asked the man if he had anything. 

His younger colleague planted himself in front of me and parroted the same question. I looked at him and felt one eyebrow raise.
