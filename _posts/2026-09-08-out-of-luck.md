date: 2026-09-08

He dressed like David Beckham, but with a red Chicago Bulls cap. The shoulder bag strapped across his chest looked like an office desk. 

He propped his mobile atop his desk and began swiping. His index finger flicked as soon as a new set of numbers rolled onto the display. He glanced to the left, then right before lighting a glass pipe. He leaned back and let out a puff of nothing.   

He zipped the phone up. Then he lifted his IKEA bag and walked towards the tunnel.
