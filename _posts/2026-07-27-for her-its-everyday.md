title: For her it's everyday
date: 2026-07-27
--- 
In the empty waiting room sat one man clutching his inner elbow. The machine assigned me a half-smudged 98. Within moments, the blinking display board summoned patient 86. 

A nurse opened the door, rested her hands on her hips, and called out "86!" Her eyes opened twice as wide as they landed on me. 

She asked about my number. I proclaimed it was 98. She laughed.
