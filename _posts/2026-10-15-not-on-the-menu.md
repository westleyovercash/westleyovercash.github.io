date: 2026-10-15

The lady in all-black sportswear sat by herself at the restaurant. Her long black hair draped down her back—all the way to her hips—in a ponytail. Atop her head rested a pair of black Bang & Olufsen headphones.

She pulled her eyes away from the screen of her phone and leaned back against the chair. She looked up toward the water-stained ceiling, let her shoulders fall, and smiled. From her left pocket she pulled out a wad of paper tissue. She pressed it against her right nostril and blasted out a *Queen Mary 2*-sized warning. Then she applied pressure to her left nostril and repeated. 

I stopped chewing and stared at her. A woman at a nearby table arched her brows and scanned over the tops of people's heads.
