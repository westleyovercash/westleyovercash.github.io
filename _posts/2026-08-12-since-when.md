I rubbed my eyebrows to coax out the right words. Then it pricked me. 

I stopped typing, and retreated to the mirror. I leaned in and then out. Then I found it: a stray brow pushing out like dry hay. 

My eyes darted down to my nostrils, then over to my ears.
