The train threw the waiting passengers in the hallway against the wall. It resisted with an ear-piercing squeal before coming to a full stop.

I pulled back the doily of a curtain crinkling its České dráhy logo. The sign at the first stop inside Slovakia displayed the name Kúty. I left the smoky, old living-room smell behind and descended the train's stairs. A trio of rail workers hopped over adjacent tracks, their shirts half untucked and an occasional belly button showing. Two old women in headscarves sat on a bench. One leaned into the other and whispered, covering her words with her hand, while the other stared at me.

The conductor blew his whistle. I watched the train disappear down the tracks.
