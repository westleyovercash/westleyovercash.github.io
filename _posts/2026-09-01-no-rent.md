They entered through the back doors of the second car in the T3 model tram—out of view of the driver or any cameras. 

The smell of days-old laundry left in a washer wafted from his oversized backpack. He took a swig of kefir, and offered her a taste. She shook her head as she slid onto his lap and wrapped her arms around his neck.

Her eyes spun around to me as she kissed him a sloppy kiss. She looked twice his age. I froze, then turned to scan the aisle for other onlookers.
