I sucked in the front half of my body so as not to ram the stroller when I came through the door. The bespectacled mother wrapped in many scarves spoke loudly to the girl in the pram while she rubbed her finger over the buttons on the queue dispenser.

I found the chair farthest away from her in the waiting room. The mother turned toward the pram. Her right hand escaped from under the layers of scarves. It held a hand mirror that, once opened, reflected her gaze back at me. She then tilted the mirror to show me her candy-pink lips. The girl in the pram begged for her lips to be painted too, but the mom told her that it would "burn like black pepper."

Their number flashed on the display overhead. The mom stood up, tossed her hair with both hands, and thrust the pram to exam room 3.
