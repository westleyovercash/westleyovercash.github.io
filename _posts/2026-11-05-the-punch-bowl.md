title: The punch bowl
date: 2026-11-05
---
I walked past the three women in the back of the #9 and grabbed on to the first pole. Two of them wore long denim dresses. The third tried to catch my eye. I took my backpack off and held it in front of me.

In the middle of the tram a man carrying an empty fishbowl and a flower sat down. As the tram rolled down the hill, he turned around and yelled, "My daughter is better than you!" in accented English.

The third woman finished painting her lips a fiery red, then pulled a mobile from her purse and aimed it at him. The man pretended to punch his fishbowl.
