title: Green Tara  
date: 2026-09-09
---
Tara was the human form of a bump on a log. The velour of her brand new green Honda cushioned what others thought of us.

Her Irish last name required freckles dotted across her face. She wore tartan skirts every day, and never left home without her oboe. While my sister made fun of my chicken legs, Tara's sister said mine were long and sexy. 

But I wanted Jeremy to talk about my legs, not them.  
 
I'd pop in the cassingle by Haddaway and blast the "What is right and what is wrong," line as we drove past the secrets of our neighbors leaving for work. She'd squeal her request for The Proclaimers instead so that the candy corn teeth duo could torture me for what felt like 500 miles. 

With "500 Miles" still on replay, and the right-hand blinker clicking minutes ahead of time, I'd wave at the crossing guard. She'd blow her whistle and nod. We'd find the lanes of parking spaces reserved for seniors. I'd move the gear to P and lift the emergency brake.
