The sun reflected off the spaceship-looking Motol metro station. I wanted to get inside before the sun melted any more of me. 

He walked in my direction: buzzed hair, scruff, and a cast on his right arm. Near him stood a mirror image with a cast on his left arm. They never noticed each other. 

I sat down in the train. My reflection looked back at me.
