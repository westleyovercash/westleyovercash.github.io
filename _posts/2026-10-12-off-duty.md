date: 2026-10-12

I looked at my watch: 04.30 and dark out. I rolled over onto my side and pulled the covers halfway over my head. 

Headlights shone on the ceiling. A diesel engine idled and whined a plea for relief. I got up and looked out the window. The Ukrainian man I often see driving taxis sat in the front passenger seat of a black Škoda. 

Through the crack in his window he let out puffs of smoke. His mobile illuminated the long swigs he took from a fist-sized bottle.
