We agreed on breakfast at 10.00. I elevatored from the suite on the top floor of Zürich's Hotel Wellenberg down to the third floor to fetch them. A hangry Peta went for a smoke while Takis dried his hair. Takis slipped on his Rolex Datejust and small handmade purse containing his pancreatic pen and a room key.

In the beige Breakfast Room, Takis and I sat next to each other. Peta planted himself across from me. Ruth's eyes widened as she bounced over to our table. "It's so good to see you again! I see you have someone new with you," she said.

"Have you found a rich husband yet?" Ruth asked, then let out a laugh as she filled our coffee cups. The older couple at the adjacent table, butter knives in hand, stared as if from behind their window at home. Peta stood up, pushed in his chair, and walked off.
