title: Gluttons
date: 2026-08-24
---

Her "hele" and "jako" peppered their way through my window. She answered the person on the other end that indeed they were finished. 

Underneath her tightly cropped, freshly dyed platinum blonde hair a pair of earbuds listened for her. Her left hand held a giant nozzle. Her right hand towed an orange machine fitting for a Dalek. Attached to the back was a trash can. 

She crossed over to my side of the street. She stuck her nozzle into all the nooks and crannies others missed.
