title: Across the table
date: 2026-11-18

Most Tuesdays they come to the French patisserie for coffee. They arrive wearing matching reflective orange vests. They place their order at the register and sit at the table in the center. It's the one least crowded by other tables.

The young man behind the register brings their order—usually a cappuccino and a tart or a madeleine. Their white canes lean against the table while off duty. The man holds his phone up a finger's width from his eye. As he scans downward, he describes something to her. She tilts her head and asks a question as he scours the display. He puts his phone down to finish his dessert, and then she slides her hand across the table and holds his. Her closed lips—like her closed eyes—start to smile.

After a half hour, they stand up and adjust their vests and small backpacks. She reaches for his elbow.
