The girl led her pack of friends into the Lidl. The boys in the back nodded and extolled her with one *sí* after the other. In her hand, she wielded a vape like a scepter.

The girl tilted her head and scanned the produce section, her eyes darting past mine. She inhaled from her scepter and released a small cloud over the bunches of grapes. I heard my heart thud in my ears. I told myself to leave this alone, like I promised everyone I would.

I cut in front of the boys and looked her in the eye. "It's illegal, and—it's just gross," I said, unable to uncrinkle my face.
