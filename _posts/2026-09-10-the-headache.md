His pony tail looked like the fingers of a lifetime smoker—the white turned a dirty, ashy orange. It covered up half of the palm trees on the back side of his bright yellow shirt. 

He chatted up the pharmacist like I've seen old men do to my Mom in grocery stores. Then he gathered the boxes of pills and paid with his Apple Watch. The terminal chirped in approval. She thanked him, and looked at me next in line. 

He didn't budge. "Do you have any of those blue pills?" he asked. I clamped my mouth to stop the giggles from escaping.
