title: Flying Dutchman
date: 2026-11-20
---
Either fate or a kind gate agent placed us in adjoining seats next to the door. Johan wouldn't have to contort himself into the seat. We were the same age. He lived near Amsterdam but would be going to university in Cape Town. 

Our KLM 747 unbuttoned the sky and let the sun out over Botswana. We scarfed down our breakfasts—Johan's blond hair almost falling into his yogurt. The plane made its first stop in Johannesburg to let people off and guzzle fuel. The flight attendant stood in the doorway, her uniform matching the color of my first African sky. As we all sat waiting, Johan laughed that it wasn't true about toilets flushing the other direction here. 

The last two seats in business class were empty. Our eyes agreed on a couple of things, but mainly that no one would notice.
