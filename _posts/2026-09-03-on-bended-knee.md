We huddled at a table outside the convention center. He lit another super slim cigarette despite the heat. 

Kamil rang him on speakerphone and plopped the iPhone on the table. From bed, the ambassador's assistant lamented about his fever and the need to switch plans. Without a chance to open my mouth, they chose me to make the formal introduction instead. My eyelids rose. 

"Sirs and dames go by their first name," I thought to myself, but ambassadors—do I bow? My shaky fingers typed it into Google.
