We checked in late that evening at the Jukebox Marriott after the winding Highway 1 drive to San Francisco. Dad shocked us all with permission to order a fancy room-service pizza.    

The next morning, the phone on the nightstand between our double beds rang. A sliver of sunlight bent around the overlapped curtains. Dad reached for the phone and listened. He drew in a breath. "Oh, she did. At what time?" he asked the deep voice on the other end. 

Mom sat up in the bed, asking who it was. Dad looked at his watch on the nightstand and said something about eastern time. Covers flew off the lump next to me. Kim wailed and ran into the bathroom.
