The three of them crossed to my side of the street. They spoke a throaty language that sounded like they needed to spit. 

Leaning against the lamppost, one held a bag of pistachios. Their hands dug in like tractors then rotated towards their mouths. They clasped each pistachio with their thumbs and index fingers.

As they split them against their teeth, the shells  released loud pops. One of them lit a cigarette.
